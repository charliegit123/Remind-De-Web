Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Remind De. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Storage

  • Local Data Storage: Remind De is designed to function offline. All your reminders, task schedules, voice notes, calendar entries, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, process, or store your personal reminders on our external servers.
  • Google Drive Backup: If you choose to use the backup and restore feature, your app data is backed up directly to your personal Google Drive account. We do not have access to your personal Google Drive files.

3. Permissions Requested

To provide core functionality, Remind De may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Alarms & Notifications: To schedule exact time-sensitive alarms and display spoken or text alerts.
  • Microphone / Audio Access: To record or preview local voice reminders and voice notes.
  • Storage / Media Access: To export or import backup files locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Remind De uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device to deliver advertisements and analyze app performance:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, or native ads). AdMob may use device identifiers, location data, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to Google's privacy policies.
  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android app integrations, in-app purchases, or API functionalities.

5. Data Security

We prioritize your data security. Since your personal reminders and app history remain on your local device or your private cloud storage, your sensitive productivity data stays fully under your control.

6. Children's Privacy

Remind De does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
