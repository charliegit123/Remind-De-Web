About the app

Remind De is an intuitive, offline-ready reminder and productivity app designed to ensure you never miss the moments, tasks, and routines that matter most.

It combines precise scheduling with spoken audio alerts, flexible repeat rules, calendar planning, and built-in meditation timers to keep your daily life organized and focused.

What's inside

Spoken voice & text reminders

Choose realistic AI voice playback, local speech synthesis, or traditional text reminders with dedicated notification chimes. Remind De speaks your reminder title aloud so you can stay informed hands-free.

Reliable exact alarms & rich notifications

Set accurate time-sensitive alarms that wake your device reliably. Manage incoming alerts from the notification tray with quick actions: close, snooze for 5 or 10 minutes, or delete.

Flexible repeat schedules

Customize reminders to fit your routine with one-time, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly recurrence. Conflict detection prevents overlapping active tasks at the exact same time.

Interactive calendar view

Plan ahead with a clean monthly calendar layout. Browse specific dates, jump to today's schedule, see indicator badges for active tasks, and create date-specific reminders instantly.

Mindful meditation timer

Take a mindful break before important tasks. Select durations from 2 to 30 minutes with circular progress tracking, start and finish audio cues, and pause or reset controls.

Comprehensive history log

Track your productivity with organized status filters. View and manage all your tasks categorized by Active, Completed, Missed, and Snoozed.

Saved voice library

Access and preview saved audio clips generated for your reminders. Replay samples or manage local voice storage at any time.

Safe backup & restore

Keep your data protected with local file exports or direct backup to Google Drive. Restore your reminders, voice notes, and settings whenever switching devices.

Thoughtful customization

Enjoy a modern Material 3 interface with full dark theme support, quick app launcher shortcuts, audio speed and volume adjustments, and fast in-app search.
